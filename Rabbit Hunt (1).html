HOW TO PLAY

  1. Watch the rabbits.
  2. Find the rabbits carrying a carrot: Level 1 has 1, Level 5 has 5.
  3. Look for the thief scarf.
  4. Move your mouse over the rabbit.
  5. Left-click (or tap) the suspected thief.
  6. Hearts give you extra time, but only when you tap them.
  7. Catch all the thieves before time runs out.
